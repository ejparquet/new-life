Kendall Pugh

Executive Director of Business

Kendall M. Pugh is Executive Director of Business at New Life Fellowship, a church founded by his parents, Dr. Maurice and Jackie Pugh. A magna cum laude graduate in Business Administration from Stephen F. Austin State University and member of Beta Gamma Sigma (top 5% of his class), he earned his MBA summa cum laude from LSU Shreveport. Kendall began his nonprofit leadership career at Uplift Community Development, where he revitalized mentoring programs and launched the Uplift Summer Leadership Academy.

At New Life Fellowship he oversees all business operations, founded the New Life Bookstore, led pandemic food distribution with Tarrant County Food Bank serving 300+ families monthly, and directs eight ministries with 100+ volunteers. Named to the 2025 Arlington 40 Under 40, Kendall is committed to serving and strengthening his community. He enjoys family time, sports, and movies.

Jackie Pugh

Executive Director of Next Gen

Jackie Pugh is the Executive Director of NextGen Ministry at New Life Fellowship Church and a passionate advocate for the spiritual development of children, youth, and young adults. A graduate of Dallas Theological Seminary with a Masters in Christian Education, she also holds degrees in Marketing and Secondary Education. For more than 20 years Jackie has dedicated her life to helping the next generation develop and grow personal relationships with Christ, ministering to children, youth, young adults, and those who serve them.

Jackie enjoys sharing the love of Christ and His Word, and she is grateful to be married to her best friend, Dr. Maurice Pugh, Senior Pastor and founder of New Life Fellowship. Together, with God’s guidance, they nurture and cherish their five young adult children: Kendall, Maurice, Natalie, and twins Shelby and Sydney. Jackie is also co‑founder and one of the hosts of the podcast Keep it Holy Sis, a Christ‑centered space for sisterhood that brings real talk, biblical truth, and encouragement to women in every stage of life.

Pastor Rodney Carter

Pastor of Worship

Rodney L. Carter is an author, global worship leader, musician, songwriter, clinician, pastor, preacher/teacher, leader, and mentor. A Chicago native, he’s recorded with world-renowned artists and has served in worship leadership ministry for over thirty years. A licensed and ordained minister of the gospel and a graduate of the University of Iowa, he holds a master’s degree in theology from Criswell College and is certified as a pastoral counselor for sexual recovery. He is married to his college sweetheart, Angela, has a teenage son, Josiah, and lives in the Dallas/Fort Worth area.

Shiretha Brown

Administrative Assistant to the Senior Pastor’s Office

Shiretha has been a faithful member of the New Life Fellowship family since the church’s early days at the YMCA as South Arlington Community Church. During that time, she served in the Children’s Ministry. After the move to the current church location, she continued serving as a Greeter and now currently serves on the Discover New Life team.

Following her retirement in 2021, Shiretha began her career with the NLF staff as the Administrative Assistant for the Groups & Growth ministry area, where she supported Pastor Eric Godfrey. She now serves as the Administrative Assistant to the Senior Pastor, Dr. Maurice Pugh. This role has provided Shiretha with a wonderful opportunity to continue serving and supporting her church family. In her spare time, Shiretha enjoys spending time with family, traveling, and attempting to start a garden.
