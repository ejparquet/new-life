Our History

In late December 2003, a spirit-led dream of Dr. Maurice Pugh came to life. That dream was to establish a church that would exemplify the ultimate church depicted in the New Testament, an Acts 2 church.

But the key to his vision was not just a religious gathering of people, but a body of believers who were genuine toward one another, genuine to their worship, and committed to sharing their faith with others to help bring unbelievers to Christ.

Pastor Pugh’s prayer became a reality on December 21st, 2003, when he and a core group of 40 adults and children held their first public worship service at an Arlington YMCA. New Life Fellowship was born.

From our humble beginnings and throughout our journey as a church body, God has demonstrated through countless moments and exciting memories that both His faithfulness and His favor were upon us.

  1. 2003: The church is born. December 21 – Established South Arlington Community Church (SACC).
  2. 2006: Room to grow. April 6 – Purchased 14 acres of land on the corner of Matlock & Sublett.
  3. 2007: A new name. June 17 – Changed church name to New Life Fellowship.
  4. 2008: Breaking ground. September 28 – Held a Groundbreaking Dedication to kick off construction. October 5 – 5-Year Church Anniversary.
  5. 2009: Moving in. August 20 – Moved into the first building of a Master Planned campus.
  6. 2013: A third service. April 7 – Added 3rd service at 11:45am. October 6 – Celebrated our 10 Year Church Anniversary.
  7. 2014: Summer Surge. June – Hosted our first Children’s VBS called Summer Surge.
  8. 2018: Fifteen years. October – Celebrated our 15 year church Anniversary.
